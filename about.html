About Me

I believe clear communication is part of treatment.

How I practise

I am Dr. Akshay Kewlani, Radiation and Clinical Oncologist in Indore. I treat with precision radiation, brachytherapy, chemotherapy and immunotherapy, planned together as a team, guided by NCCN and international guidelines.

My special interests are head and neck, neuro-oncology, breast and gynecologic cancers. My promise is simple: you will always know what we are doing, why, and what to expect next.

Beyond the clinic

I explain cancer, radiation and fitness in simple language on YouTube and Instagram, for patients and for students considering oncology.
